# SDE branding: logo, application icons and user pictures

This page describes the visual identity of SDE 0.2: the official logo and its
variants, the icon family of the SDE applications and the sample user
pictures, where each one is used and how the files are made.

**SDE and DebION are separate projects.** SDE, the Selviana Desktop
Environment, is the desktop environment made by the Selviana DE Project.
DebION GNU/Linux is a separate distribution, based on Debian testing, that
ships SDE. Each has its own artwork: the SDE logo and icons are described
here; DebION's logos, wallpaper, boot splash and login background are in
`data/desktop-base/` (package `debion-desktop-base`, made by
`tools/make-debion-artwork.py`). Do not combine the two logos into one mark.

Selviana Desktop Environment, SDE and the SDE logo are trademarks of the
Selviana DE Project. The copyright license of the files (GPL-3.0-or-later,
see `debian/copyright` and [LEGAL.md](LEGAL.md)) does not grant rights to
the name and the logo; this page describes how they may be used.

## The SDE logo

The official artwork is a retro CRT monitor (cream body, thick black outlines,
inner bezel, power button, three small buttons, green LED and a knob) whose
screen shows a black line-art portrait between red, green and blue stripes,
above the wordmark **SELVIANA** (heavy sans, black outline, red→green→blue
gradient) and the tagline **DESKTOP ENVIRONMENT**.

`docs/branding/sde-logo-official.jpg` is the official file, kept unchanged as
the reference. It is a JPEG whose "transparent" background is a baked-in
checkerboard and it carries a footer of legal text. **The footer text is not
part of the UI artwork**: it is cropped away and never reproduced (its claims
are being reviewed separately).

### Files

| File | What it is | Use it on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data/branding/sde-logo.png` / `.svg` | Monitor, SELVIANA and DESKTOP ENVIRONMENT, 1600 px wide | light backgrounds |
| `data/branding/sde-logo-dark.png` / `.svg` | The same with a near-white tagline (#F4F4F2); everything else unchanged | dark backgrounds |
| `data/branding/sde-mark.png` / `.svg` | The monitor alone, 1024 px square (icon framing) | any background |
| icon `sde-logo` (hicolor 16–512 px and scalable) | The mark as an icon; at 16, 22, 24 and 32 px a hand-simplified, pixel-aligned drawing of the same monitor (cream body, black outline, light screen, RGB stripes, portrait silhouette) | icon slots |

They are installed to `/usr/share/sde/branding/` and
`/usr/share/icons/hicolor/*/apps/sde-logo.{png,svg}` (package `sde`). The
About windows, Control Center → About and Welcome load `sde-logo`,
`sde-logo-dark` (dark themes) and `sde-mark` from there — SVG before PNG,
`$SDE_BRANDING_DIR` first — and fall back to the `sde-logo` icon.

The PNGs combine the cleaned original colours (with their soft shading)
under the crisp traced ink; the SVGs are faithful vector traces (black ink
layer, flat colour layers and the sampled wordmark gradient). Both come from
the official file and are regenerated by `tools/make-sde-logo.py`, which:

1. crops the footer away;
2. removes the checkerboard. The fake transparency is a grid of 48 px grey
   and white cells; every cell is measured, per region between ink lines,
   and every edge pixel is un-premultiplied against it. Outside the monitor
   and in the letter counters the result is true alpha with clean
   anti-aliased edges; on the screen the checkerboard under the portrait, the
   stripes and their edges is replaced by an opaque light screen colour
   (#F4F3EE), so the black portrait stays legible on dark backgrounds;
3. traces the ink with potrace and the flat colours, and samples the
   wordmark gradient.

It needs python3-numpy, python3-scipy, python3-pil, potrace and
rsvg-convert. Nothing else about the artwork was changed: same face, same
lettering, same colours.

### Where the logo is used

Only in important places:

- the About windows of the SDE applications;
- Control Center → About;
- Welcome;
- the SDE session entry of the login screen (`/usr/share/xsessions`,
  `Icon=sde-logo`);
- the README.

Panels, menus, launchers and dialogs use the application icons below, not the
logo.

### Clear space and minimum size

- Keep clear space on every side of at least **one tenth of the logo's
  width** (the PNGs include a small transparent margin, not the full clear
  space). Nothing — text, other logos, edges of a window — goes inside it.
- Full logo with the wordmark: at least **200 px wide** on screen (about
  30 mm in print); below that the tagline is not legible, use the mark.
- Mark: at least **48 px**. Below 48 px use the `sde-logo` icon, whose
  16–32 px sizes are drawn for those sizes.

### Light and dark backgrounds

Use `sde-logo` on light backgrounds and `sde-logo-dark` on dark ones; the
only difference is the tagline colour. The monitor keeps its black outlines
and its light screen on every background, and the mark works on light, mid
grey and dark backgrounds alike. On photographs, place the logo on a calm,
even area.

### Do not

- recolour any part of it, invert it, or make a single-colour version on your
  own;
- stretch, squash, rotate, skew or crop it;
- re-letter it, change the typeface, translate or retype the wordmark, or
  move the tagline;
- redraw the portrait, replace it with another face or remove the stripes;
- add shadows, glows, outlines or other effects;
- add the footer text of the official file, or any other text, to it;
- use the old teal "S" tile that SDE 0.1 shipped as `sde-logo`.

## Application icons

One family derived from the logo: friendly rounded shapes, flat colours with
a soft top-light at most, cream and soft-neutral bodies and the logo's red,
green and blue as calm accents. Every icon has its own silhouette and
dominant colour, a consistent charcoal outline (#2B2B2F) and no text.

| `niny` | Niny, text editor | notepad with lines and a pencil | blue |

They are generated copies, not separate drawings.

All of them are installed as hicolor icons at 16, 22, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 and
256 px (and 512 px for `sde-logo`) plus scalable SVG, in package `sde`;
dh_icons refreshes the icon cache on installation.

### Drawing rules

- **Master** (`data/icons/hicolor/scalable/apps/NAME.svg`): 256 px grid with
  about 16 px of padding, outline 7 units (3.5 px at 128 px), inner lines
  5 units. It is the scalable icon and the source of the 128 and 256 px PNGs.
  Outlines carry `class="outline"` and inner lines `class="detail"`: at 48
  and 64 px `tools/render-icons.py` draws them 1.5× and 1.3× wider, so the
  outline is about 2 px there.
- **Small sizes** (`artwork/icons/16|24|32/NAME.svg`): hand-simplified
  drawings with 1 px outlines on the pixel grid. The 24 px drawing keeps a
  1 px margin; the 22 px icon is that drawing without the margin.
- Palette: outline #2B2B2F; the logo's cream (#E6E0D4, lighter #ECE6DA) and
  side grey (#ADA79A); calm, desaturated accents close to the SDE theme
  accents (`data/themes/palettes.json`), for example red #C9564E, green
  #4A9157, blue #3876B9 and teal #2B807B — not the logo's neon stripe
  colours, which only the logo and the `sde-logo` icon use.
- No letters (the `>_` prompt is the only symbol), no copies of other icon
  themes, no third-party logos or trademarks.

### Editing

Edit the SVG (master or small size), run `tools/render-icons.py` and commit
the SVG together with the regenerated PNGs; package builds use the committed
PNGs and do not need the renderer. The script uses `rsvg-convert`
(librsvg2-bin) or, when it is missing, builds `tools/svg2png.c` against
librsvg and cairo. Review the result on light and dark backgrounds at 16,
24, 32, 48 and 128 px (see the contact sheet below) before committing.
