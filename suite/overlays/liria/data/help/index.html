The window

The header row holds Back, Forward and Parent Folder, the path of the current folder, the view switcher (icons, list, compact), Search and the main menu. Click a part of the path to go there, or right-click it for its menu. To type a location, click the empty part of the path, click the pencil button or press Ctrl+L; Escape brings the path back. The small arrows at the ends of the path appear only when the path is too long for the window and scroll it. Files dropped on a part of the path are moved or copied into that folder.

The sidebar shows Home, then Pinned (the same pins as Quick access, with a pin next to the item under the pointer), Bookmarks, Drives and Network, with the colour icons of your icon theme. Click a heading to collapse or expand its section; Liria remembers it. Drop a folder on the Pinned heading, or between two pinned items, to pin it there; drop files on a place to move or copy them into it. Press F9 to show or hide the sidebar.

Ribbon

Below the header, the ribbon groups the file commands in tabs. Home: Pin to Quick access, Copy, Paste, Cut, Copy path, Paste shortcut (links to the copied files), Move to, Copy to, Delete, Rename, New folder, New document, Properties, Open, Open with, Select all, Select none and Invert selection. Share: Email (attaches the selected files), Compress, Copy to drive and Make link. View: navigation pane, extra pane, status bar, icons, details or compact view, icon size, Sort by, hidden and backup files and the ribbon mode. A Drive tab appears in folders of a drive that can be ejected, and an Archive tab (Extract here, Extract to) when only archives are selected. Buttons that do not apply to the selection are greyed out.

The ribbon has three modes, chosen in Main Menu › Ribbon (also under View), with the Ribbon button of the View tab or with the arrow at the end of the tabs: the full Ribbon, Ribbon Tabs Only (click a tab to drop its commands down; they close after a command, with Escape or when you click the files) and Compact Header (No Ribbon). Ctrl+F1 switches between the full ribbon and tabs only. The choice applies to every Liria window and is kept in ~/.config/sde/liria.conf. On small screens the ribbon starts with tabs only.

Home

Your home folder opens on the Home view with Quick access, Drives (cards with their free space) and Recent files; click a section heading to collapse it. Hide overview shows the files of the home folder instead, and Show overview brings Home back.

Quick access holds what you pin: folders, documents, pictures, archives, server locations or folders on a USB drive. Pinned cards carry a small pin. Folders you use often (where your recent files are) follow as Frequent folder cards; pin one with the pin button that appears over it or with Pin to Quick Access in its menu. Pin an item with Pin to Quick Access in its right-click menu (in the file view or the sidebar), with the ribbon, by dropping it on the Quick access area or with the + card. A folder opens in the window; a file opens in its usual application. Right-click a card (or press Shift+F10) to rename it, move it left or right, unpin it or reset Quick access to the default folders. Drag a card to move it, Delete unpins the focused card, F2 renames it and Ctrl+Shift+Left/Right moves it. Items that were moved, deleted or are on a drive that is not connected are dimmed and marked Not available. The list is kept in ~/.config/sde/liria-quick-access, one location per line, and every open window follows changes to it.

Drag and drop

Drag files and folders between Liria windows, tabs and panes, onto places and drives in the sidebar, onto a part of the path, onto folder and drive cards of the Home view, and to or from other applications. On the same drive files are moved, to another drive they are copied: hold Shift to move, Ctrl to copy or Ctrl+Shift to make links. Drag with the right (or middle) mouse button to choose Move Here, Copy Here, Link Here or Cancel when you drop. A folder dropped on the Quick access area (not on a card) or on the sidebar's Pinned heading is pinned; files dropped on a folder card or a drive card go into that folder or drive. Cards of the Home view can be dragged into folders and other applications too.

Menus

The main menu (F10) opens new windows and tabs, a terminal in the current folder, connections to servers, hidden files, sorting, view options, bookmarks, preferences, keyboard shortcuts and this help. Right-click a file for Open, Open With, Cut, Copy, Rename, Copy To, Move To, Send To a removable drive, Compress or Extract, Move to Trash and Properties. Right-click the background of a folder to create folders and documents or to paste.

Selecting files by name

Press Ctrl+S and type a pattern: * matches any text and ? one character, for example *.jpg or report-??.pdf.

Opening a location

Press Ctrl+L and type a folder path such as /media, ~ for your home folder, or a place on another computer: \\server\share or smb://server/share for a Windows share (written as in Windows), an IP address or a computer name such as 192.168.1.10, nas or localhost for its Windows shares (the other protocols are suggested while you type), sftp://user@server/folder (or ssh://), ftp://, nfs://, davs:// and dav:// (WebDAV), or smb: for the Windows network. The servers you opened are suggested as you type.

Bookmarks

Press Ctrl+D to bookmark the current folder; it appears in the sidebar and under Main Menu › Bookmarks. Ctrl+B edits, renames or removes bookmarks.

Removable drives

Copies to USB sticks and memory cards are written through to the drive, so the progress shows what really reached the device, and the operation ends with Finishing while the last data is written. Names that FAT, exFAT or NTFS cannot store are adjusted, and files of 4 GB or more are refused on FAT32 with a clear message. Use Send To in a file's menu to copy it to a drive. Always use Eject or Safely Remove in the sidebar before unplugging: Liria first writes all cached data to the drive.

Connecting to a server

Type the address in the location bar (see Opening a location), or use Main Menu › Connect to Server for Windows shares (SMB), SSH (SFTP), WebDAV, FTP and NFS. When a server asks you to sign in, Liria asks for the user name, the domain and the password, or lets you in as a guest; Remember the password in the keyring is offered when a keyring runs in your session. Liria remembers the servers you opened, never their passwords. A new SSH server asks once whether to trust its key. FTP and WebDAV without HTTPS send passwords unencrypted, and Liria says so. If this computer cannot open a protocol, Liria names the package to ask your administrator for (gvfs-backends). The connection settings are the system's own (Samba and SSH configuration). Mounted servers appear in the sidebar under Network.

File properties

Press Alt+Return to see a file's size, type, location, dates, permissions and the application that opens it.

Backgrounds and emblems

Emblems mark files and folders with small symbols. The desktop wallpaper is set in SDE Settings, or with Set as Wallpaper in a picture's menu.

Preferences

Main Menu › Preferences sets the default view and icon size, whether one or two clicks open items, what happens with executable text files and the trash, and whether the Delete command bypasses the trash.

Display: the information shown under icon names and the date format.

List Columns: the columns of the list view and their order.

Preview: thumbnails, item counts and previews for local and remote files.

Media: what happens when a disc, camera or drive is inserted.

Keyboard shortcuts

Press Ctrl+? in Liria for the complete list. The most used: Ctrl+T new tab, Ctrl+F search, Ctrl+H hidden files, Ctrl+1/2/3 icons/list/compact view, F2 rename, Delete move to trash, F3 extra pane, Ctrl+F1 collapse or expand the ribbon.
